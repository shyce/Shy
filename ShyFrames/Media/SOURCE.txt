Where the files in this folder came from.

The name font is someone else's, passed on unchanged. Everything else was
made for ShyFrames. The bars' fill is ShyCore's (Shy.Skin.HATCH).

BigNoodleTitling-Oblique.ttf
    The name font.
    By James Arboghast / Sentinel Type, 2003. Free to use and to pass on with
    its documentation intact; not to be sold, renamed or altered without the
    author's permission. Its notice, as the font carries it, is beside it:
    BigNoodleTitling-README.txt.

ShyFrames.tga, ShyFramesSmall.tga and their Mono twins
    The addon's icons. Original work.

RiserFill.tga, RiserEdge.tga
    The housing on a plate's top edge, for the buff check and combo points.
Blade.tga, BladeWell.tga, BladeGlow.tga
    A combo point: a blade, its empty well and its glow.
    All five are original work.

Numbers are set in ShyCore\Media\NunitoExtraBold.ttf.
