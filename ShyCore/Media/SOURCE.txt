Where the files in this folder came from.

Hatch.tga, HatchTall.tga
    The fill of every bar that shows an amount (Shy.Skin.HATCH, and
    Shy.Skin.HATCH_TALL for the tall plaque): a rounded bar's light crossed
    by a weave of fine diagonal lines, one every 6 pixels, deepest through
    the middle. 252 x 32 (60 rows tall), grey, seamless where it repeats.
    Original work, drawn from numbers.

LilitaOne.ttf
    The display font, Latin only, for Shy's own words. SIL Open Font
    License: OFL.txt beside it.

NunitoExtraBold.ttf
    The rounded font, for names and numbers: Nunito at weight 800, a
    static cut of its variable font (github.com/google/fonts, ofl/nunito).
    SIL Open Font License: OFL-Nunito.txt beside it. In Lua it is
    Shy.FONT_ROUNDED.

NunitoSansText.ttf, NunitoSansTextLarge.ttf
    The text font, for running text such as chat lines: Nunito Sans,
    semi-condensed, at weight 600 for sizes under 16 and 540 from 16 up
    (400 is regular, 700 bold). Static cuts of Nunito Sans' variable font
    (github.com/google/fonts, ofl/nunitosans), under the SIL Open Font
    License: OFL-NunitoSans.txt beside them. In Lua they are Shy.FONT_TEXT
    and Shy.FONT_TEXT_LARGE, and Shy.UI.TextFont picks between them.

    The rounded and text fonts also carry every character a player can
    type that isn't Chinese or Korean: Greek, Thai, more Latin and
    Cyrillic, symbols, arrows, box drawing and one-color emoji, drawn in
    from the Noto fonts (github.com/google/fonts: ofl/notosans,
    notosansthai, notosanssymbols, notosanssymbols2, notosansmath,
    notosansmono, notoemoji), under the SIL Open Font License:
    OFL-Noto.txt beside them. Chinese and Korean come from the game's own
    fonts through Shy.UI.NameFont.

Everything else (the icons, Bar, Frame, FrameGlow, Well, PanelEdge, Bracket,
Cap, Facet, Rib, Gloss, Glow, Sheen, Grip, GripLock, the slot, hover and
glyph art)
    Original to this addon.
